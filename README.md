# Pixel Tower Defense — Support

**Pixel Tower Defense** is a retro pixel-art tower defense game for iPhone. Build Archer, Cannon, Poison and Tesla towers, drag Kai onto the road to block enemies, cast Frost Trap and Grenade, earn coins and buy new guns in the Armory, and protect the princess across 5 maps and 50 levels.

## How to play

- **Build:** tap a stone slot and choose a tower. Tap a tower to upgrade it (up to level 3) or sell it.
- **Move Kai:** put your finger on Kai and drag. He follows your finger and stops where you let go.
- **Block:** stand Kai on the road. Up to 3 ground enemies stop and fight him.
- **Spells:** tap the snowflake (Frost Trap) or the grenade, then tap the map.
- **Waves** start on their own; tap **SKIP** to call the next one sooner.
- **Coins:** every level pays coins. Spend them in the **Armory** on the title screen.
- **Stars:** keep your hearts. 9–10 hearts left = 3 stars.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the game need an internet connection?** No. It is fully offline.

**Where is my progress saved?** On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

**Are there ads or in-app purchases?** No.
