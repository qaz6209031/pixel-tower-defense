# Pixel Tower Defense — Privacy Policy

*Effective October 18, 2026*

Pixel Tower Defense ("the app") is made by Kai-Chin Huang. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## We do not collect any data

- The app does **not** collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.
- The app has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking**, and contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- The app works fully **offline** and does not connect to the internet.

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, weapons) and settings (sound, screen shake, game speed) are saved **only on your device**. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
